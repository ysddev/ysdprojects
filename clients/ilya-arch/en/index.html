Projects

Every house starts with the site and with how people will live in it.

Approach

We start with the site, the light and the way you live. The form comes later.

We take away everything unnecessary until a precise, calm house remains.

We run the project ourselves, from the sketch to the last detail on site.

How we work

From the first meeting to handover a house usually takes a year to a year and a half.

  1. 1

    First meeting

    We meet, visit the site and discuss the brief, timing and budget.

  2. 2

    Concept

    Floor plans, massing and first visualisations, developed together with you.

  3. 3

    Working drawings

    Drawings, details and specifications a builder can follow without guessing.

  4. 4

    Interior

    Light, finishes and furniture in the same language as the architecture.

  5. 5

    Build and handover

    Design supervision on site until the day you move in.
